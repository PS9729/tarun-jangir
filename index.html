Harshita E-Mitra Kendra

ई-मित्र केंद्र

We are an authorized Rajasthan Government E-Mitra service center providing 500+ G2C services to citizens since 2016. Our center is recognized by the Department of Information Technology & Communication, Government of Rajasthan.

We bridge the digital divide by providing government services to all citizens, especially those who are not comfortable with online procedures. Our trained staff assists in filling forms, document preparation, and following up with government departments.
